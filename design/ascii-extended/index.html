bismuth ascii — extended surfaces

Three app surfaces the original redesign handoff never specified: the terminal, the sheet + drawing chrome, and the export view. Each is a retheme onto the ASCII direction's existing tokens — no new primitives, no new controls.

Terminal

xterm.js retheme. The 16 ANSI slots are derived from the scope's own tokens rather than authored per theme, so all four scopes come for free. Bracket tab chrome, underline _ caret.

app/src/TerminalView.tsx · design/guidelines/view-terminal.card.html · PORTING.md §1

Sheets · Drawing

Chrome only. The Univer canvas and the drawing canvas are untouched — what changes is the formula bar, the headers, the sheet tabs, the tool row, and the paper ground. Brush strokes stay real ink; they are the one surface in the system that is not on the character grid.

app/src/{SheetView,DrawView}.tsx · design/guidelines/view-sheets-draw.card.html · PORTING.md §2

Export

A pure retheme of ExportView — same grid, same fields, same order, same chips. The rendered artifact carries its own copy of the chosen scope's tokens, and sits on 22px ruled paper.
